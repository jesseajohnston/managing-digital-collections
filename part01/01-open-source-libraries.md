## On Source

The _source_ refers to source code. However,
the concept of dependencies can complicate this.

As noted by Safiya Noble, the choices made by big tech in developing
and promulgating platforms has a significant impact on
what information users can ultimately access and search [@noble2018].

And Bustillos describes the often unconventional and antagonistic
social positions of the GNU community [@bustillos2015].

It's interesting to note the different ways that open source
projects can be organized, from what seems to be an essentially
anarchic refusal of organization to policy-focused ways of codifying
and formalizing open architectures through existing organizational formations [@reagle2013].[^n1]
