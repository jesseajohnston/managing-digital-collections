title: "Lab 04: Gather Five Collection Items"
---

In this lab, you will create "artisanal metadata" for five collection items.
These items will be reused in your initial CB sites and Omeka sites.

## Process

First, you need to identify the five items you want to include.
Although it is your choice as the creator, I would suggest choosing a subset of an existing collection,
such as five things from the ["Free to Use Libraries" set from the Library of Congress](https://www.loc.gov/free-to-use/libraries/).

Next, you need to gather the metadata for these items and gather at least one representative file for each item.

Finally, "collect" these in your SI 676 github repo.

## Lab 4: Deliverables

- A CSV with the metadata for your items
- Digital images corresponding to your items
- Submit both by uploading to your course github repo in a folder titled `lab-04`, provide the URL via the Canvas assignment
